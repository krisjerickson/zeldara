## What the numbers say

| Measure | What good looks like |
|---|---|
| Mobile day-1 return | top quarter > 30 %, top tenth ≈ 40 % (GameAnalytics 2026) |
| Mobile day-7 / day-30 | median under 4 % / ≈ 0.7 % |
| PC session | median ≈ 18 min |
| Web game start | under 10 s and under 20 MB first download (CrazyGames); 46 % of web players have quit a slow game (Poki) |
| Web → bought | 62 % of Poki players have later bought a game they first played on the web |
| Forgiving streaks | a 7-day streak: 3.6 × likelier to finish (Duolingo); one missed day does not break a habit (Lally 2010) |
| A head start | 34 % finish vs 19 % (Nunes & Drèze 2006) |
| Ending on a high | 18 vs 6 chose to replay (Gutwin et al., CHI 2016) |

## The game today

**What already pulls**

- The painted world, the story, the seasons, the villagers with their own arcs, and the twin.
- Four difficulty levels, and lessons that can be turned off.
- The first minutes are taught by doing.

**Where it rubs**

- **Saving is manual** (round 47). A crash or a closed tab can cost a whole site.
- **The first load is the whole game**, ≈ 390 MB.
- **31 lessons each pause the game.**
- **Death costs 10 % of your gold with nothing given back.**
- **No gamepad.**
- **Nothing to share**, and no second player.
- **Nothing tells you how long a site takes.**
- **No play data at all**, so every change is a guess.

## Seven theses

1. **Lower the door.** Start in seconds, play on anything, never ask twice.
2. **Competence first.** Inside five minutes the player gets a win, a find and a thank-you.
3. **Respect the evening.** Each sitting has one goal and lasts about 20 minutes. It ends on a high, and nothing is ever lost.
4. **A world that reads itself.** Landmarks pull you on, the map remembers, and one thread is lit at a time.
5. **A village that remembers you.** Every return shows change, and even dying tells you something.
6. **A rhythm, never a leash.** There are reasons to come back, but missing a day costs nothing and nothing for sale wins the game.
7. **Made to be shared.** Every peak leaves a painting, a card, a code, or a second controller.

## The ethical line

No loot boxes, no energy timers, nothing for sale that wins, and no punishment for a missed day. Play data is opt-in and off for children.

The game's strongest pull is the village, the twins and the painted world. The proposals lean on those rather than on compulsion loops (Zagal, Björk & Lewis 2013, dark patterns).
