# Round 47 notes — the empty space, the E, the heavy axe, saving

## 1. What fills the empty space (by floor — Kris's card)

| Floor | Fill | Moves | Creatures |
|---|---|---|---|
| Castles, and the ground floor of every tower | the world's own ground round the site (the region's texture), with its trees, rocks and plants | with the floor | — |
| Underground (dungeons, crypts) | rock: strata, cracks, roots, ore glints | with the floor | moles, rock worms, beetles |
| Chasm sites | an abyss: a far-down dark with three banks of fog | slower than the floor (depth) | bats |
| Lava sites | a sea of lava with crust plates | drifting | lava and rock gargoyles, magma serpents |
| Upper tower floors | the element's sea: fire → lava, water → deep sea, earth → clouds, storm → storm clouds, shadow → stars and aurora, grass → treetops | slower than the floor | fins, tentacles, sky whales, storm eels, shooting stars |

Framing keeps the camera to the floor and a vignette softens the corners. All of it is drawn now; wave 50 adds the painted versions (textures, creatures, fog banks, clouds and peaks, the night sky).

## 3. Combat

- Axe: 0.9 s a swing, stand still for 0.35 s, 1.6× damage, stagger. Damage per second is a little below the sword's, in exchange for big single hits.
- Spells: bolts seek the strongest enemy within ~60° ahead. The world cooldown bug (spells worked once in the overworld) is fixed.
- Bosses: 2× health; faster by region (×1, ×1.15, ×1.30, ×1.45).

## 4. Saving and quick use

- Save buttons in the Inventory and the Tome; milestones and sleep save by themselves; nothing else does.
- Quick use row in the inventory: the food O eats, the potion P drinks, the ammo the bow uses.
