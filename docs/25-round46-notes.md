# Round 46 notes — empty space, the E, where to help with lag

## 1. The empty (black) space round the new floors

Kris: "There's quite a lot of "black" or empty space in some of the new designs … Propose ideas to run by me". Six ideas, each shown round a real floor in Lab → **Empty space** (pick any number; they can differ by kind of site):

| Idea | What it looks like | Fits |
|---|---|---|
| Carved out of the mountain | the void becomes rock: strata, cracks, roots, ore veins | dungeons, caves, castles |
| Open sky beyond the walls | clouds drifting below, far peaks and towers, birds | towers, cloisters, sky sites |
| A deep drop with depth | fog layers moving at different speeds, hanging chains and roots, falling dust | chasms, mage towers |
| The site's own element all round | lava sea, dark water, a sea of clouds, a starry void — moving | elemental sites, island crypts |
| Night sky and aurora | deep starfield, slow aurora bands, a distant planet, shooting stars | mage towers |
| Frame the floor tightly | the camera keeps to the walkable part; a soft vignette at the edges | any, alongside the others |
