# The forty spirits — where they came from

Kris (Lab › Backstories for everyone else, the forty spirits, A): five lines each, from their own realm's lost towns — "make sure that it
isn't implied that all of the spirits came from the village. OK that they know people from the village, but they usually came from a
different quadrant and the lost cities from there."

Each realm had its towns before the gate shut, and each emptied in its own way:

- **The Grasslands** — Fernby (farms and dye-pools at the crossroads), Stonewick (the fishing town above Mirror Lake), Hollowmere (orchards,
  hives and carving sheds), the Barrow Downs (where the dead were sung up the Tree). Emptied slowly, in the Long Quiet.
- **The Wetlands** — Millbrook (the great mill on the Silverrun), Reedhaven (the potters and midwives of the reed-beds), Hverholm (the town
  behind the dikes), the Lantern Isles (bell-towers on the water). Drowned in one night, the Black Tide.
- **The Highlands** — Deepdelve (the deepest mine-town), Kettlecrag (the mountain lakes and their high moor), Highmark (the last town on the
  cliff road to the western sea). Broken by the Stone-Fall and the splitting of the Great Scar.
- **The Ashlands** — Askvald (the greatest of the ember-cities, with its skalds' hall), Brandhavn (its harbour, where gems were cut), Emberhold
  (under the Wall). Burned in one night, the Ash Night.

Only Hallveig, the cooper, lived in the last village (she is Rolf's wife); Geir carved the Green's tree as a boy on a visit and went home to
Hollowmere. The rest know the village only by name, or through someone who walked there once. Freed, they gather on the Runestone Green
until their soul-lines bring them home — and there, [Tab] near them, one of them talks.
