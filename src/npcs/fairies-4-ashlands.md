# Ember
key: ember
who: Ember

realm: 4

kind: cinder pixie
spirits: Mord, Njal
## Backstory
Ember is Cinderheim fairy who stayed lit. She kept the stone at the gate of Askvald, the greatest of the ember-cities, and on the Ash Night
she watched it go in a single night of red sky and then grey. She burned as hot as she could to keep the ash off her stone, and has never
let herself go dim since — which is exhausting, and which she will never admit. Her two are Mord, a boy from Askvald who remembers only
that the sky was red, then grey, and then he was here, and Njal, a young stonemason who was building the city wall on the night the Ember
Wall rose — and was on the wrong side of it.
## Before
- Mord was a boy in Askvald. He remembers red, then grey, then chains. That is all. I fill in the rest for him, gently. I leave out the worst.
- Njal was on the wrong side of the Ember Wall when it rose. His family was on the other side. In Gildenmark. Alive, he hopes.
- I burn as bright as I can, all night, every night, so the ash cannot settle on them. If I go dim, the ash comes. I do not go dim.
- Askvald had nine gates and a hundred towers. I kept the stone at the first gate. There are no gates now. There is ash.
- If Njal asks you about Stonewick, in Gildenmark — tell him the truth, whatever it is. He has waited long enough.
## After
- Mord asked what the sky looks like now. I told him to look. It was blue. He did not know skies could be blue. He has not stopped looking up.
- Njal went to Gildenmark to look for his family. Bramble knew them. She flew him there herself.
- I let myself go dim last night for the first time in twenty-eight winters. I slept for two days. The ash did not come. There is no more ash to come.
- Askvald is still ash. But there is a green thing growing at the first gate. One. Mord found it. He guards it like a dragon.
- They do not need me burning any more. I am trying to learn how to just be warm.
## Life
- I am Ember because I stayed lit. That is the whole story. It took twenty-eight winters to tell.
- I am tired in a way that sleep does not fix. I think it is the kind of tired that only stops when you are not needed. I hope I am not needed soon.
- Cinder says I show off. Cinder is right. Somebody had to be the brightest thing in Cinderheim.
- I have never seen a green field. Ashe has never seen anything green at all. I have seen one moss. I tell her about it.
- Askvald's bells were bronze. They melted. I have a drop of one, cooled, in my stone. It still rings if you breathe on it.
## Stage 2
- I heard there is a waystone in Fernheim with a fairy who rang bells under water. I would like to meet her. We have a lot in common.
## Stage 3
- The ash on the plain is thinner. Underneath it is grey earth, not black. Grey is a start.
## Stage 4
- The Ember Wall is broken. I flew through the gap. On the other side: grass. All of it. Everywhere. I had to sit down.
## Stage 5
- Askvald has a gate again. Wooden. Small. Mord built it with Njal. The green thing has become a tree. Mord still guards it.

# Cinder
key: cinder
who: Cinder
group: fairies
realm: 4
fairy: 1
kind: cinder pixie
spirits: Orm, Ranveig
## Backstory
Cinder is sharp-tongued, quick and proud, and she kept the stone at Brandhavn, the ember-city's harbour, where ships brought gems in from
every realm to be cut. Her two are Orm, a midwife — the only man among Cinderheim's midwives — who was at a birth on the Ash Night when
the babe never cried, and Ranveig, a potter who heard Surtharn say that a god had promised him the gems of Runeheim. Cinder argues with both
of them constantly, because arguing is how cinder-pixies show love, and because if she stopped she would have to think about the babe.
## Before
- Orm was at a birth on the Ash Night. The babe never cried. He has carried that for twenty-eight winters. Do not make him tell it twice.
- Ranveig heard the fire-giant say a god had promised him the gems. She is a potter. Potters notice what people say over a kiln. Ask her.
- I argue with them every night. About pots. About the weather. About whether a Binder has ever had a bath. It keeps them talking.
- Brandhavn was the harbour where the gems came in. Every realm's gems. Every ship. Now the harbour is glass.
- I am not sad. I am cross. There is a difference. Sad sits down. Cross gets up.
## After
- Orm walked to Gildenmark and sat outside the midwives' house for a long time. Nanna from Fernheim sat with him. Neither of them spoke.
- Ranveig told you about the gems. Good. Now she says she wants to make something beautiful out of clay again, something nobody wants to buy.
- I have nobody to argue with. I tried arguing with the stone. It agreed with everything. It was terrible.
- Orm asked me if he did everything right, that night. I told him yes. I have never been so sure of anything.
- Brandhavn's harbour glass is cracking. Underneath there is water. Real water. Ranveig says a harbour is just water with patience.
## Life
- I am Cinder: what is left after the fire, still hot. Touch me and find out.
- Cinder-pixies show love by arguing. If I have not argued with you, I do not like you yet. Give it time.
- Ember shows off. I tell her so every day. That is love too.
- Brandhavn had a thousand ships. I knew the name of every one. Some of them were very silly names.
- I am afraid of nothing. Except water. And the dark. And being forgotten. That is three things. I said nothing. I am not good at counting.
## Stage 2
- Fernheim fairies sent a message by heron. It says "hello" and "the bells are ringing for you". The heron was rude. I argued with it. It was lovely.
## Stage 3
- Ranveig made a bowl. It is crooked and lovely and nobody wants it. She gave it to me. I sleep in it.
## Stage 4
- The fire-giant's forges are cold. The gems are going home to their realms. Ranveig watched the first cart go. She said, "Promised. Ha."
## Stage 5
- Orm caught a babe in Brandhavn's new houses last week. She cried at once, loud enough to wake the harbour. Orm cried louder.

# Ashe
key: ashe
who: Ashe
group: fairies
realm: 4
fairy: 2
kind: cinder pixie
spirits: Sverri, Thora
## Backstory
Ashe was born on the Ash Night, inside the Ember Wall itself, from a spark that would not go out, and she has never in her life seen a green
thing. Everything she knows of the world she read in a skald's book that fell into the ash, which she has read nine hundred times. She keeps
the stone under the Wall near Emberhold, and her two are Sverri, an old shepherd who saw two lights go out above the clouds that night,
like a pair of eyes closing, and Thora, a smith who forged the Binders' chains because they made her — and then wore them. Ashe asks them
about the world before; they are the only teachers she has ever had.
## Before
- Sverri saw two lights go out above the clouds on the Ash Night. Like eyes closing, he says. He is old. He sees clearly.
- Thora forged the chains. They made her. Then they put them on her. She does not want to be forgiven. Forgive her anyway.
- I was born that night, in the Wall, from a spark. I have never seen green. Sverri describes grass to me. I think he is exaggerating.
- I read a skald's book nine hundred times. I know all about moss. I want to touch it before anyone tells me what it is like.
- Emberhold stood right under the Wall. Now it is part of the Wall. Thora says some of the town is in there still. I try not to think about it.
- Do not tell me what green is. I want to see it first.
## After
- Thora took the chains off herself. Just held them, and they fell. She dropped them in the ash and walked away and did not look back. Good.
- Sverri looked at the sky and said, "The lights will open again." I asked what lights. He just smiled.
- They want to show me Gildenmark. I am afraid. What if green is not what the book said?
- Thora is forging again. Hinges and nails. "Things that hold doors open," she says. "Not shut."
- I went through the Wall. I saw it. Green. I touched moss. It is soft. The book was right. I did not cry. I did. A lot.
## Life
- I was born inside a wall of fire. My first sound was a crackle. I am told most fairies start with a song.
- I have read one book. Nine hundred times. It is about a skald who loses his harp. He finds it on page two hundred and six. I cry every time.
- Ember is the brightest. Cinder is the crossest. I am the newest. I do not know what that makes me yet.
- I am made of ash. When I am sad I go grey. When I am happy I go grey with sparks.
- Green. I want to see green. If I see green I will know the world is real and not just a book.
## Stage 2
- A heron flew over the Wall today. It was grey and white and very rude. It was the first thing I have ever seen from the outside.
## Stage 3
- Sverri is teaching me what sheep are. They are clouds that walk, he says. He is exaggerating. I hope he is not.
## Stage 4
- I have seen green now. I go through the gap in the Wall every morning to look at it. It is still there every time. I check.
## Stage 5
- Moss is growing on my stone. On my stone. In Cinderheim. I am not going to scrape it off. Primrose says I must. Primrose is wrong.

# Soot
key: soot
who: Soot
group: fairies
realm: 4
fairy: 3
kind: cinder pixie
spirits: Unn, Vali
## Backstory
Soot is Cinderheim's quiet watcher — small, dark, almost invisible against the ash, which is exactly how she likes it. She kept the stone in
the forest that burned outside Askvald, where only black trunks stand, and she has spent the dark years listening, because nobody notices a
smudge of soot and everybody says things in front of it. Her two are Unn, a young forester who learned that Muspelheim and the sky are tied,
and Vali, a brewer who once saw two thrones in a feast hall and now sees none. Between them they know a great deal; Soot knows more, and
will tell you a little of it if you are very patient.
## Before
- Unn knows something important about the fire and the sky. Ask her. She will say it once. Listen once.
- Vali brewed for a feast hall once. Two thrones there were. Now none, and two moons inside, he says. I do not understand it. I remember it.
- Nobody notices soot. I sit on the Binders' shoulders and listen. They talk about the fire-giant's temper. It is very bad.
- The forest outside Askvald burned in one night. The trunks are still standing, black. I keep my stone among them. It is very quiet.
- The Binder at their stone is afraid of birds. I do not know why. I have watched him flinch at a sparrow. Use it.
## After
- Unn walked into the burnt forest and found one seedling. A pine. She sat with it all night.
- Vali brewed the first ale in Cinderheim for twenty-eight winters. It tasted of smoke. He says the next one will taste of barley.
- I do not have to listen to Binders any more. I listen to birds. They talk about much nicer things. Mostly worms.
- Unn told you the thing about the fire and the sky? Good. Then you know what to do. I have nothing to add. That is a first.
- When they rose, the black trunks creaked. As if the forest remembered being a forest. I think it did.
## Life
- I am Soot. I am the smudge on the stone nobody looks at twice. That is how I learn things.
- I know more than the other fairies. I do not say so. Saying so would make them notice me.
- I sat on a Binder's shoulder for a whole winter once. He never knew. He talked to his chains. He was very lonely. I almost felt sorry for him.
- The burnt forest is beautiful at dawn. Black trunks and pink sky. Nobody else thinks so. That is fine. It is mine.
- I am not shy. I am careful. There is a difference, and it has kept me alive.
## Stage 2
- I heard the Binders talking. They are frightened of you. I thought you should know. I thought you should not let it go to your head.
## Stage 3
- Unn's pine seedling is knee-high to a fairy. That is tall for a fairy. Do not laugh.
## Stage 4
- The fire-giant's servants are leaving Cinderheim. I watched them go. Some of them looked back. One waved at me. I did not wave back. I nearly did.
## Stage 5
- The burnt forest is green at the bottom now. The black trunks are still standing, but there are birds in them. They talk about worms. I love it.

# Pyra
key: pyra
who: Pyra
group: fairies
realm: 4
fairy: 4
kind: cinder pixie
spirits: Alfhild, Brand
## Backstory
Pyra is Cinderheim's fierce storyteller, a flicker of a fairy who kept the stone by the skalds' hall in Askvald, where the sagas were sung
every winter night. Her two are Alfhild, a skald who sang that when the cycle breaks two will come from the water to mend it — and thought it
was only a song — and Brand, an old baker who wants you to ask the Lady of the Fields where the spring went, if you ever find her. Pyra
believes in prophecies with her whole small burning heart, and has spent twenty-eight winters making Alfhild sing the water-song again and
again, waiting for it to come true.
## Before
- Alfhild sang that when the cycle breaks, two will come from the water to mend it. She thought it was only a song. I never did.
- Brand says to ask the Lady of the Fields where the spring went. I do not know who the Lady is. He does. He will not tell me. He smiles.
- I make Alfhild sing the water-song every night. She is tired of it. I am not. Two from the water. I believe it.
- The skalds' hall in Askvald had a hearth the size of a house. The sagas were sung there every winter night. I knew them all. I still do.
- Brand baked for the skalds. He says a saga is only as good as the bread at it. He is not wrong.
## After
- Alfhild looked at you when she rose. Just looked. Then she said, "Well. It was not only a song." I have never been so happy.
- Brand asked me if anyone has found the Lady of the Fields yet. I said not yet. He said, "Soon." He seems very sure. Old bakers usually are.
- I made Alfhild sing the water-song one more time. She sang it differently. Slower. As if she was singing it to someone.
- The skalds' hall is ash, but Alfhild sang in the ruin of it, and the ash listened. You should have heard it.
- Two from the water. I knew. I always knew. I am going to be insufferable about it for a hundred years.
## Life
- I am Pyra, the fire-story. I burn brightest when someone is listening.
- I believe in prophecies. Not because they are true, but because people make them come true by believing them. Is that not the same thing?
- Ember stays lit. Cinder argues. Ashe reads. Soot listens. I tell. Between us we are a whole saga.
- I know every saga sung in Askvald. When the hall burned I sang them all to myself so none would be lost. It took three nights.
- My favourite saga is the one where nobody dies. It is very short. It is the best one.
## Stage 2
- Alfhild has started a new verse. She will not let me hear it until it is finished. I have never been so impatient in my life.
## Stage 3
- Brand made bread in a stone oven in the ruins. The smell went all the way to the Ember Wall. The other fairies came to see what it was.
## Stage 4
- Alfhild's new verse is about you. It is very long. It is not finished. She says the ending is up to you.
## Stage 5
- Brand found the Lady of the Fields. He asked her where the spring went. She laughed and pointed at everything. He has not stopped smiling since.
