# Mord
key: sp_mord
who: Mord of Askvald
group: spirits
realm: 4
kind: a boy of the ember-city
## Backstory
A boy of Askvald, the greatest of the ember-cities, who remembers only that it was night, that the sky was red and then grey — and then the
stone. Ember stayed lit beside him so he would never be in the dark.
## Life
- Askvald had glass windows in every house. At sunset the whole city lit up orange like a lantern. I thought every city in the world did that.
- My father cut gems. He let me hold the rough ones. They looked like dirty pebbles until he polished them. Then they looked like stars.
- It was night, but the sky was red, and then it was grey, and then I was here. That is all of it. I do not remember being scared. I remember being cold.
- Ember never let herself go dim, not once, so I could see. She is very tired, I think. Somebody should tell her she can rest now.
- I want to see a green thing. Ashe says she has never seen one either. We are going to look for one together.

# Njal
key: sp_njal
who: Njal of Emberhold
group: spirits
realm: 4
kind: a carter
## Backstory
A carter of Emberhold who drove ore wagons between the mines and the ember-cities. On the Ash Night the Ember Wall rose out of the ground in one
night, and he was on the wrong side of it.
## Life
- I drove the ore road for twenty years. Emberhold to Askvald and back, two days each way. I knew every rut by its sound.
- The Wall came up out of the ground in one night. Black rock, steaming. My wagon was on the outside. My family was on the inside.
- I drove along it for three days looking for a gap. There was no gap. Then the Binders found me first.
- Ember says the Wall has a pass in it now. A gate with a road through. I am going to walk through it the slow way and look at every stone.
- If my horses had any sense they ran north. I like to think of them grazing in the Highlands, very old and very fat.

# Orm
key: sp_orm
who: Orm, the midwife of Brandhavn
group: spirits
realm: 4
kind: a midwife
## Backstory
A midwife of Brandhavn, the ember-city's harbour — the only man among the Ashlands' midwives. On the Ash Night he was at a birth, and he does
not speak of how it ended.
## Life
- People laughed at a man midwife. Then they needed one at three in the morning and stopped laughing.
- In Brandhavn I knew every family on the harbour. The gem-cutters, the sailors, the fishmongers. All of them called me "the big hands".
- That night I was working. The gate was already shut. I will not say more than that. Cinder knows. That is enough.
- Cinder argues with me every day. About the weather, the harbour, the best fish. I think it is how she keeps me talking.
- Nanna of Reedhaven was a midwife too. I would like to meet her. We could talk shop. Nobody else ever wants to talk shop with a midwife.

# Ranveig
key: sp_ranveig
who: Ranveig, the potter of Brandhavn
group: spirits
realm: 4
kind: a potter
## Backstory
A potter of Brandhavn whose kiln stood by the harbour gem-houses. She heard Surtharn say, in his own voice, that a god had promised him the gems
of Runeheim — and the Binders chained her for having heard it.
## Life
- Brandhavn clay is red and the kilns burn hotter than anywhere. My glazes came out of the fire looking like little sunsets.
- The gem-ships came in from every realm. Blue from the lakes, green from the marsh, white from the mountains. Brandhavn cut them all.
- I heard him. Surtharn. He said a god had promised him every gem in Runeheim. I did not believe gods made promises like that.
- Cinder says I talk too much. I say she argues too much. We are both right and we both enjoy it.
- When I am home I am going to make a bowl with one gem from every realm set into the rim. All four. To remember they belong together.

# Sverri
key: sp_sverri
who: Sverri, the shepherd of Emberhold
group: spirits
realm: 4
kind: a shepherd
## Backstory
An old shepherd of Emberhold, under the Wall, who kept black-faced sheep on the warm slopes. On the Ash Night he saw two lights go out above the
clouds, like a pair of eyes closing.
## Life
- Ash-sheep have black faces and grey wool, and the wool is warm as a fire. Emberhold cloaks were the warmest in Runeheim.
- The slopes under the mountains were always warm from below. My sheep never needed a barn. They just lay down on the ground in winter.
- That night I looked up and saw two lights above the clouds go out. One, then the other. Like eyes closing. I have never told anyone but Ashe.
- Ashe was born on the Ash Night out of a spark. She has never seen grass. I describe it to her every day. She says I make it sound made up.
- If there is a single ash-sheep left in the Ashlands, I will find her. They are very good at hiding in the grey.

# Thora
key: sp_thora
who: Thora, the smith of Emberhold
group: spirits
realm: 4
kind: a smith
## Backstory
A smith of Emberhold, where the ore came out of the ground half-melted. The Binders made the smiths of Emberhold forge their chains — and then
put those chains on them.
## Life
- Emberhold iron came out of the mountain already warm. We barely needed a fire. The best blades in Runeheim were born there.
- They came with gold and orders. Chains, they said. A thousand chains. We forged them because the alternative was being chained.
- Then they put them on us. Our own work. I knew every link I had made. I could feel my own hammer-marks on my ankle.
- Ashe asked me what a forge is for. I said for making things. She asked what things. I said, for a while, chains. I am ashamed of that answer.
- Garrick in the last village and Dunn the dwarf — I hear they are the best smiths left. I would like to show them how to break a chain from the inside.

# Unn
key: sp_unn
who: Unn, the forester of Askvald
group: spirits
realm: 4
kind: a forester
## Backstory
A young forester of the woods outside Askvald, where now only black trunks stand. Chained in the burned forest, she learned from the Binders'
talk that Muspelheim and the sky are tied.
## Life
- The Askvald woods were red pines with bark like copper. In the evening the light came through them like through coloured glass.
- The forest burned in one night. Every tree. They are still standing, black, like they are waiting for someone to tell them it is over.
- Muspelheim and the sky are tied. Break the fire and the sky will fall to you. I heard the Binders say it. They were afraid when they said it.
- Soot listened with me. Nobody notices soot. Nobody noticed me either, chained to a black tree. We heard everything.
- I am going to plant the first new pine in the Askvald woods. Unn the forester, planting again. I like the sound of that.

# Vali
key: sp_vali
who: Vali, the brewer of Askvald
group: spirits
realm: 4
kind: a brewer
## Backstory
A brewer of Askvald who carried his ale to the great feasts in the far hall in the years before the gate shut. He remembers two thrones there,
and the night they were gone.
## Life
- Askvald ale was black and bitter and you drank it hot. Nobody outside the Ashlands could stand it. That was the point.
- Once a year I carried a cask up to the great feast. A long road. Worth every step. The hall was so bright you could not look at the roof.
- There were two thrones in the feast hall once. Now there are none, and two moons inside. I do not know what it means. I know it is wrong.
- Soot kept me company. She is so quiet that sometimes I forgot she was there, and then she would sneeze and I would jump out of my chains.
- Ulf from the Lantern Isles brews mead, they tell me. Mead! I will teach him ale. Proper ale. Black and bitter and hot.

# Alfhild
key: sp_alfhild
who: Alfhild, the skald of Askvald
group: spirits
realm: 4
kind: a skald
## Backstory
A skald of the hall in Askvald where the sagas were sung every winter night. She sang that when the cycle breaks, two will come from the water to
mend it — and thought it was only a song.
## Life
- The skalds' hall in Askvald had a hearth as long as a ship. Every winter night we sang, and the ember-city listened through the windows.
- A skald must know every saga, but she is allowed one favourite. Mine was about a mountain that learned to fly. It never happened. It is still my favourite.
- I sang the old verse a thousand times and never once thought about what it meant. That is the trouble with songs. They sound like songs.
- Pyra tells the stories now, with sparks. She is better at it than I was. Do not tell her that. She already knows.
- Vigdis of Millbrook was the best skald of the Wetlands. I would like to sing with her. Two skalds, one hall, every saga twice.

# Brand
key: sp_brand
who: Brand, the baker of Askvald
group: spirits
realm: 4
kind: a baker
## Backstory
An old baker of Askvald whose ovens were heated by the warm ground. He asks every traveller the same thing: if you find the Lady of the Fields,
ask her where the spring went.
## Life
- In Askvald you could bake bread by burying it in the warm ground for a night. I did it properly, in an oven. I had standards.
- Ash-bread is dark and a little smoky. The traders from the Grasslands said it tasted like a campfire. I took it as a compliment.
- Ask the Lady of the Fields where the spring went. If you ever find her. I have been asking for twenty-eight winters. Nobody has answered.
- Pyra tells my story with sparks and I come out much braver than I was. I let her. A man can be braver in a story.
- Yrsa of Millbrook and Benno in the last village — bakers, they tell me. Three bakers, three ovens, one long table. That is my idea of heaven.
