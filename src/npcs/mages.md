# Frostwhisper
key: mg_rime_spire
who: Frostwhisper the Rime Witch
group: mages
realm: 1
title: The Rime Spire
## Backstory
Frostwhisper kept the winter herbs for the Grasslands farms: she knew which frost made the sweetest turnips and which would kill a lamb. When
the gate shut she climbed her spire with the Frost Bolt and pulled the ladder up after her, meaning to wait one winter for someone worthy.
The winter never ended in her head. Thirty-six years later the frost still creeps from her feet because she has forgotten how to stop it.
## Scene
Frostwhisper as she was thirty-six winters ago: a young woman in a farm kitchen in Fernby, frost-blue shawl, laughing, holding up a
frost-sweetened turnip to a farmer's family around the table; a little frost pattern on the window behind her like lace.
## After
- The frost stops at my feet now. Look. I had forgotten that it could. Take the Frost Bolt — and tell the Fernby farms the turnips will be sweet this year.

# Quillon
key: mg_scriptorium
who: Magister Quillon the Arcane Scribe
group: mages
realm: 1
title: The Scriptorium Tower
## Backstory
Quillon was the realm's record-keeper; his book wrote down whatever was true in Runeheim, by itself. When the gate shut the book got stuck
on one page — the page that says the road up is closed — and wrote it again every day. Quillon locked himself in with it, guarding the Sky
Storm for "the next true thing", and has been reading the same page for thirty-six years.
## Scene
Quillon before the dark: a younger bearded wizard in violet at a high window of a tower full of floating books, the self-writing book open
before him, filling with bright new lines as a sunrise floods the Grasslands below.
## After
- My book has turned a page. For the first time in thirty-six years. Look — it is writing about you. Take the spell. I would like to read something new.

# Cindra
key: mg_hearthfire
who: Cindra the Hearth-Witch
group: mages
realm: 1
title: Hearthfire Tower
## Backstory
Cindra was the village witch everybody went to with burns and broken hearts; her cauldron was never cold and her door never shut. On the
Ash Night she saw the red sky in the west and understood what fire could become. She shut her door for the first time in her life and has
guarded the Fireball since, sure that anyone who wanted it wanted it for the wrong reasons — until someone came who fought her fairly.
## Scene
Cindra before the dark: a red-hooded village witch at an open cottage door at dusk, her cauldron glowing inside, a line of villagers
waiting with bandaged hands and baskets of thanks; warm hearth-light spilling out across the snow.
## After
- You fought fair and you fought hard. Fire is a hearth before it is a weapon. Remember it, and take the Fireball. My door is open again.

# Bramblebeard
key: mg_rootspire
who: Old Bramblebeard the Thorn Druid
group: mages
realm: 1
title: The Rootspire
## Backstory
Bramblebeard planted half the hedges of the Grasslands, and stood so long in his grove after the gate shut — listening for a season that
never came — that his beard grew into the brambles. He kept the Thorn spell because thorns protect what grows, and nothing was growing.
## Scene
Bramblebeard before the dark: an old druid planting a hedge along a Grasslands lane in spring, children of the farm handing him saplings,
blossom on every bush; his beard long and white and only a little tangled with leaves.
## After
- There. Do you feel that? Something in the roots. A season, coming. I have waited thirty-six years to feel it. Take the Thorns — they guard growing things.

# Voltara
key: mg_conductor
who: Voltara the Storm-Caller
group: mages
realm: 2
title: The Conductor's Spire
## Backstory
Voltara called the rain for the Wetland mills and turned the storms away from the harvest. On the night of the Black Tide she called every
storm she had to push the river back — and failed; the Tide came up from below. She has stood on her spire ever since with her hair full of
lightning, guarding the Arc Lightning, unable to put the storm down.
## Scene
Voltara before the dark: a storm sorceress on a mill-tower roof above the Silverrun, gently drawing a summer rain over golden Wetland
fields, millers waving from below, rainbows over the river.
## After
- I can put it down. The storm. I did not know I could. Take the Arc Lightning — and call rain with it, sometimes, not only thunder.

# Glacius
key: mg_frozen_belfry
who: Glacius the Shard Sorcerer
group: mages
realm: 2
title: The Frozen Belfry
## Backstory
Glacius rang the great belfry of Hverholm at every wedding and every burial. When the Tide drowned the town the bell froze mid-swing, and
so did he — a sorcerer of ice who could not bear to let the last bell finish ringing. He guards the Ice Storm in the frozen tower, keeping
everything exactly as it was the moment before.
## Scene
Glacius before the dark: a young hooded sorcerer in a belfry ringing a great bronze bell for a wedding below in Hverholm, the town's
dike-top streets full of people, flower petals in the air, frost sparkling only a little at his fingertips.
## After
- The bell finished its swing. Did you hear it? Thirty-six years it has been waiting to ring. Take the Ice Storm. I am going to let things melt now.

# Mother Morrow
key: mg_mire_tower
who: Mother Morrow the Bog Hex-Witch
group: mages
realm: 2
title: The Mire Tower
## Backstory
Mother Morrow cursed cheats and blessed honest boats for every market on the Silverrun, and was the least trusted and most needed woman in
the Wetlands. When the Tide came she hexed the Binders who came for her dead, and held them off for a year before the marsh took her
tower. She guards the Thunder Storm and trusts nobody — least of all anybody polite.
## Scene
Mother Morrow before the dark: a crooked bog-witch at a busy floating market on the Silverrun, glaring at a cheating merchant while blessing
a fisher's boat with a sprig of bog-myrtle; lanterns on the water, laughter all around.
## After
- You hit me fair and you did not say sorry. Good. I trust you now. Take the Thunder Storm — and if Quillon asks, I never stole his quill.

# Marenus
key: mg_drowned_lighthouse
who: Tidelord Marenus the Sea-Warlock
group: mages
realm: 2
title: The Drowned Lighthouse
## Backstory
Marenus kept the lighthouse on the Lantern Isles, and his light brought home every boat on the lake for forty years. The Tide drowned his
lighthouse with its lamp still burning, and he went down with it, a lighthouse-keeper who would not leave his post. He guards the Tidal
spell under the water and still trims a lamp nobody can see.
## Scene
Marenus before the dark: a broad-shouldered keeper at the top of a tall lighthouse on the Lantern Isles at night, trimming the great lamp
whose beam sweeps across Mirror Lake, little boats with lanterns turning for home along it.
## After
- The lamp is out. I can stop now. Thirty-six years I kept it lit under the water. Take the Tidal spell — and if you see a boat on the lake at night, wave it home for me.

# Pyrrhia

group: mages
realm: 3
title: The Ember Observatory
## Backstory
Pyrrhia watched the stars from the highest observatory in the Highlands and wrote down every new light in the sky. On the night the gate
shut she watched two lights go out above the clouds and wrote it down, and never wrote another line. She keeps the Flame Wave and a sky-map
that ends on that night.
## Scene
Pyrrhia before the dark: a star-gazing mage at a great brass telescope in a domed observatory on a Highland peak, tiny suns orbiting her
staff, apprentices around her pointing at a sky crowded with stars and a bright white city high in the clouds.
## After
- I can see new stars again. Small ones. Coming back. I will start the map again tonight. Take the Flame Wave.

## Backstory
Hiemal was the Ashlands' cooler: an ice-mage who kept Askvald's forges from burning the city, beloved in a land of fire. On the Ash Night he
put every bit of cold he had around the city — and it was not enough. He froze himself on his throne with the Frost Storm rather than feel
the heat again.
## Scene
Hiemal before the dark: a tall ice-mage in a simple crown of frost walking the hot streets of an ember-city at noon, smiths and children
gathering round him as cool snow drifts from his hands; red forges and towers behind.
## After
- It is cold, and it is enough. For the first time it is enough. Take the Frost Storm. Use it to cool things, not to stop them.

# Astraeus
key: mg_astral_spire
who: Astraeus the Star Sorcerer
group: mages
realm: 4
title: The Astral Spire
## Backstory
Astraeus was the oldest mage in Runeheim, who sewed real stars into his robe — one for each child born in the Ashlands, so their light
would be kept. On the Ash Night the stars on his robe went cold one by one. He guards the Inferno in his dreaming loft, counting them.
## Scene
Astraeus before the dark: an ancient star-sorcerer in a midnight robe sewn with glowing stars, sitting by a cradle in an Ashland home,
gently sewing one new bright star onto his sleeve while the parents watch in the firelight.
## After
- One of the stars on my robe is warm again. Only one. But it is warm. Take the Inferno. I will keep counting — upward now.
